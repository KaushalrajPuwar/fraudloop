What am I looking at?

What gets scored: one card transaction, described by eight numbers — amount, hour of day, how many transactions this card made in the last 24 hours, its 30-day average amount, distance from home, whether the merchant is foreign, a merchant risk score, and how old the card is.

How: a logistic regression turns those eight numbers into a single fraud probability between 0 and 1. The three features that pushed the number furthest are shown under every decision.

BLOCK means the probability is at or above 0.50 — in a real system that would decline the payment or send it to a human for review. ALLOW means below 0.50 and the payment would proceed.

Why things move: the page re-reads /stats every 3 seconds. The counters only change when a transaction is actually scored — by the button below, or by a load generator if one is running. Nothing is stored in a database; the permanent record is in Kibana.

If it feels busy: press Freeze. The numbers stop updating and you can read them at your own pace.
